## Diagnosis
- **Thin content.** About 450 words of body text, 4 short H2s, "Leitura: 4 min". Competitors (seuguiadetecnologia, mundodosreviews, buscamelhores, my-best) publish comparisons with models, prices and specs.
- **Wrong format for the intent.** The query "câmera pet com dispensador de petisco" is commercial: people want to see models. The page only discusses "vale a pena" in general terms and names no product apart from a passing mention of the Newpet 2K.
- **Blind CTA.** The top and bottom buttons go to `meli.la/1buR4DT`, "Dispensador Inteligente De Petiscos - Monitoramento E Câmera" at R$1.069 (monitor, 14/11). The product is not named, there is no price, and there is no photo. The reader clicks on a product they never saw.
- No product schema or ItemList. FAQ has 4 questions. dateModified is 22/08. Only the hero image.
- Internal links: 5 posts link to this page. It links to the camera hub and to the comparison.
- Cannibalization: low. `melhor-camera-para-monitorar-pet` is the general ranking. This page can own the "com dispensador / que joga petisco" niche.

## SERP / market
Models that appear in Brazil:

- KaBuM! Smart 500 (Full HD, Alexa/Google, 1-year warranty, cheaper);
- Furbo 360° (premium, for dogs);
- Petcube Bites (limited availability in Brazil);
- generic Tuya models on ML;
- the model already linked (R$1.069).

## Recommendation
Rewrite with the same pattern as the comedouro and bebedouro rankings:
- Title: "Câmera Pet com Dispensador de Petisco: 5 Melhores de 2026 (Vale a Pena?)".
- Answer-first opening, picks, comparison table, 4 to 6 product cards with photo, price, specs, pros and cons, and real reviews.
- Keep the "vale a pena / quando é custo extra" section, plus the petisco-type and comedouro-difference sections.
- New H2s: "Câmera que joga petisco funciona com gato?", "Que petisco usar no dispensador?", "Câmera com petisco x câmera comum: qual escolher?"
- FAQ of about 10 questions, ItemList/Product/Offer, top CTA naming the product, links to the camera hub and the comparison.
- Needs from the user: a folder with 4 to 6 products (meli.la link + screenshot of each listing).
